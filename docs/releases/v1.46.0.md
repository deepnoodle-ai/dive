## Highlights

Dive v1.46.0 adds Google's Gemini 4 Neon (`google.ModelGemini4Neon`, also
available on OpenRouter as `openrouter.ModelGemini4Neon`), positioned between
Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Hydrogen in the recommended Google lineup. Pricing
($3.00/$15.00 per 1M tokens, $0.15 cache read) and live-probed reasoning
capabilities are included out of the box.

Gemini 4 Neon is the first Gemini model to accept `xhigh` and `max` thinking
levels in addition to `low`, `medium`, and `high`. The Google provider now
forwards those levels for models whose capability entry lists them, while
continuing to clamp existing and unlisted Gemini models at `high` so callers
targeting older or preview IDs see no change in behavior.

DeepInfra's catalog also catches up with the latest open-weight and third-party
frontier releases, adding `Qwen/Qwen4-Max`, `moonshotai/Kimi-K3.1-Pro`,
`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`, `deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4-Pro`, and
`MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-M3`. The `dive` CLI recommended list for DeepInfra now
surfaces `Qwen4-Max`, `Kimi-K3.1-Pro`, and `DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash` in place of
their predecessors, while older model constants remain available.
